Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 9, 2026

What we collect

JCG Fitness stores the information needed to provide nutrition tracking: account details, profile and goal information, meal, water and weight logs, budget preferences, and community content you choose to post. If you use the food scanner, the photo you choose is sent for analysis. If you use the AI coach, the message you send is processed to provide a response.

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate your account, show your progress, save and synchronize logs, provide requested food analysis and recommendations, operate community features, and improve reliability. Nutrition estimates and AI responses are informational and may need correction.

Where it is processed

Account and app data are processed through JCG Fitness services and its configured cloud providers. Scanner and chat requests are sent only when you choose those features. Do not include sensitive personal or medical information in community posts or AI messages.

Your choices

You can edit your tracked entries in the app. You may stop using optional scanner, AI, and community features at any time. To request account or data assistance, contact the developer at keikoocatalasan@gmail.com.

Security and changes

We use authenticated access and service controls intended to protect app data. No internet service is risk-free. We may update this notice when the app changes; the updated date above will reflect the current version.
