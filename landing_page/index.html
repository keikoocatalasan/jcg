01 — features

A premium dashboard designed to provide granular tracking with AI-guided assistance.

AI Food Scanner

Snap a photo of your meal. The on-device camera feeds our backend AI to instantly detect dishes and output precise calorie, macro, and price estimations tailored to local Filipino foods.

Budget-Aware Planning

Achieve your fitness goals without overspending. The recommendation engine scores meals deterministically based on their nutritional density relative to their cost in the local market.

AI Nutrition Coach

Need advice on budget meal-prep? Talk with the embedded chatbot. It parses your profile history and gives personalized, safe advice while adhering strictly to safety guardrails.

Offline-First Design

No internet connection? No problem. Track your meals, hydration, and logs locally using our SQLite database. Changes are queued and sync securely with Supabase once you're online.
